# Trump didn't accuse Ukraine of election interference after strikes on Russian 'bot farms,' despite claims

Claim:
U.S. President Donald Trump accused Ukraine of midterm election interference after it struck “bot farms” in Russia in October 2026.

Because the rumor about Trump's accusation against Ukraine spread beyond the Betoota Advocate's website without visual signs — such as disclaimers or watermarks — indicating that it was satire, we find it originated as satire.
The Betoota Advocate first shared the rumor on Oct. 14.
It reported that Trump "accused Ukraine of election interference by bombing bot farms in Russia earlier this week, leading to a sharp decrease [in] social media activity worldwide" while speaking to reporters aboard Air Force One.
We found no record of Trump speaking to reporters aboard Air Force One on Oct. 14. Trump did
