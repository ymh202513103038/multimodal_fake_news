# Fake image depicting real attack on protester at Trump rally spreads online

Claim:
An image authentically shows attendees at one of U.S. President Donald Trump's rallies punching the face of a protester wearing a shirt that says "RELEASE THE EPSTEIN FILES."
Rating:
Fake
About this rating
In October 2026, social media users spread an image that claimed to show a woman being punched in the face at one of U.S. President Donald Trump's rallies.
The person in the picture was wearing a shirt that said "RELEASE THE EPSTEIN FILES." (The Justice Department has
released millions of files
related to its investigation of the late sex offender Jeffrey Epstein, but
critics allege
the agency made redactions to obscure information.)
The image circulated on platforms
such as X
and
Facebook
. Many users sharing the image appeared to believe it was authentic.
"
If there's one photo to sum up the Trump era, this is it," one
X post
said.
While a protester wearing a similar shirt
was, in fact, attacked
at an Oct. 12 Trump rally in Youngstown, Ohio, the image above was created using generative artificial intelligence, according to OpenAI's detection software. As such, we have rated this image as fake.
OpenAI's verification tool
detected an invisible watermark on the image called SynthID, which the company's AI tools, like ChatGPT, embed in generated images. (As such, the service can only detect images generated with OpenAI's software and does not detect AI generation via other large-language models, such as Google's Gemini.)
(OpenAI)
SynthID detectors are largely accurate but can still be fallible. However, a review of credible
footage
taken from the Trump rally indicated that the actual protester was wearing slightly different clothing, including an open flannel layered on top of the T-shirt. The T-shirt the real person wore also had variations in the lettering style and coloring, unlike the more uniform version in the AI-generated image.
A reverse-image search could not determine the original creator behind the AI-generated picture.
Numerous reputable news organizations, including
NBC News
and an ABC
affiliate
, have published legitimate footage of the event.
NBC News'
video
shows rally attendees chanting, "USA, USA, USA" as a man in a gray shirt and a baseball cap appears to push down the protester. Another man in a dark blue or black shirt appears to tackle the protester, hit her and put
her
in a headlock. Security officials then escort the protester away.
