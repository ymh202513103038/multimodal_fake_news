# ‘Fake News' Trump Clashes With Kaitlan Collins, Calls 'Treasonous'

President Donald Trump clashed with reporter Kaitlan Collins during a White House press conference after being pressed about the ongoing U.S. military campaign against Iran. Defending his administration's actions, Trump accused the "fake news" media of wanting America to lose the conflict and called such coverage "really treasonous."
