# Trump calls CNN gutless losers. "It's all garbage. It's all fake news."

President Trump in true Trump form Tuesday morning calling out what he calls the “Fake News” media.

As he left the White House, Trump responded to questions about the war between Israel and Iran, focusing on CNN in particular saying, "You're gutless losers.” I say that to CNN because I watch it. I have no choice. I've gotta watch that garbage. It's all garbage. It's all fake news. But I think CNN is a gutless group of people."

He also went after MSNBC saying they both downplayed the success of the United States strike on Iranian nuclear facilities, Fordow, Natanz and Isfahan.
